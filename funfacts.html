01 / PHYSICS

Lightning Is Hotter Than the Sun's Surface!

A lightning bolt can heat the air around it to temperatures much higher than the surface of the Sun!

The air in a lightning channel can reach around 30,000°C. This sudden heating causes the air to expand rapidly, creating the sound we hear as thunder.

The Sun's visible surface, called the photosphere, is about 5,500°C. That means lightning can briefly heat the surrounding air to several times that temperature!

02 / CHEMISTRY

Water Can Exist in Three States!

Water is one of the few everyday substances we commonly see as a solid, liquid and gas.

Ice is the solid form of water. Liquid water is what we drink, and water vapour is its gaseous form.

These states depend on temperature and pressure. Water can change from one state to another through processes such as melting, freezing and evaporation.

Under special conditions, water can even exist as ice, liquid water and water vapour together at its triple point.

03 / BIOLOGY

Some Plants Eat Insects!

Not all plants get their nutrients in the same way. Some fascinating plants can trap insects!

The Venus flytrap is a carnivorous plant. Its leaves have special structures that can close when an insect touches trigger hairs inside them.

These plants still make their own food through photosynthesis. Trapping insects helps them obtain certain nutrients, especially in nutrient-poor environments.

Other carnivorous plants, such as pitcher plants, use different structures to trap insects.

04 / ASTRONOMY

There Are Stars Beyond Counting!

The universe is so enormous that the number of stars is difficult to imagine. Our Sun is just one star among many!

The Sun belongs to the Milky Way galaxy, which contains a huge number of stars. Our galaxy is only one of many galaxies in the observable universe.

Some stars are much larger than the Sun, while others are smaller. Stars also have different colours depending partly on their surface temperatures.

When we look at distant galaxies, we are seeing light that travelled for a very long time before reaching us.
