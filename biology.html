ARTICLE 01 • THE BASICS

What Exactly Is Biology?

Look around you. The trees, birds, insects, animals and even you are all part of the living world. But what makes something alive? That's exactly what biology helps us understand!

Biology is the branch of science that studies living organisms and their interactions with the environment. It explores everything from microscopic bacteria to enormous animals.

Biology also helps us understand how living things grow, reproduce, obtain energy and adapt to their surroundings. It is divided into several branches, including botany, the study of plants, and zoology, the study of animals.

ARTICLE 02 • BUILDING BLOCKS OF LIFE

The Amazing World of Cells

Imagine a tiny world that exists inside every living organism! It is too small to see with our naked eyes, yet it performs some of the most important functions needed for life. Welcome to the world of cells!

A cell is the basic structural and functional unit of life. All living organisms are made up of cells. Some organisms, such as bacteria, consist of just one cell, while humans, plants and most animals are made up of many cells.

Cells contain different structures called organelles, each performing a specific job. The nucleus controls many cell activities, the cell membrane regulates what enters and leaves the cell, and the cytoplasm contains many of the cell's components.

Plant cells also have a cell wall, chloroplasts and a large central vacuole.

ARTICLE 03 • THE WORLD OF PLANTS

How Do Plants Make Their Food?

Unlike humans and animals, green plants do not need to search for food. They have a remarkable ability to prepare their own food using sunlight. This process is called photosynthesis!

Photosynthesis is the process by which green plants prepare food using carbon dioxide and water in the presence of sunlight. Chlorophyll, the green pigment found in chloroplasts, helps plants absorb sunlight.

During this process, plants produce glucose, which they use as food, and release oxygen into the atmosphere. This oxygen is essential for the survival of many living organisms.

Photosynthesis is one of the most important processes on Earth because it supports food chains and helps maintain the balance of gases in the atmosphere.

ARTICLE 04 • THE HUMAN BODY

Inside the Human Body

Your body is like an extraordinary machine! Every second, different organs and organ systems work together to keep you alive. But how does everything work so perfectly?

The human body is made up of several organ systems. Each system has a particular function, but they all work together to maintain life.

The circulatory system transports blood, oxygen and nutrients throughout the body. The respiratory system helps us breathe, while the digestive system breaks down food into simpler substances that the body can use.

The nervous system controls and coordinates body activities. It allows us to think, respond to our surroundings and control different body functions.
